4.2.2. Power down SIM800H&SIM800L 
 
SIM800H&SIM800L will be powered down in the following situations： 
z Normal power down procedure: power down SIM800H&SIM800L by the PWRKEY pin.  
z Normal power down procedure: power down SIM800H&SIM800L by AT command “AT+CPOWD=1”.  
z Abnormal power down: over-voltage or under-voltage automatic power down. 
z Abnormal power down: over-temperature or under-temperature automatic power down. 
4.2.2.1. Power down SIM800H&SIM800L by the PWRKEY Pin
