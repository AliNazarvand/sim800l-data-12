4.8.2. SIM Card Design Guide 
 
SIM card signal could be interferenced by some high frequency signal, it is strongly recommended to follow 
these guidelines while designing: 
 
z SIM card holder should be far away from GSM antenna 
z SIM traces should keep away from RF lines, VBAT and high-speed signal lines 
z The traces should be as short as possible 
z Keep SIM card holder’s GND connect to main ground directly 
z Shielding the SIM card signal by ground well  
z Recommended to place a 100nF capacitor on SIM_VDD line and keep close to the SIM card holder 
z Add some TVS which parasitic capacitance should not exceed 50pF 
z Add 51Ω resistor to (SIM_RST/SIM_CLK/SIM_DATA) signal could enhance ESD protection 
 
4.8.3. Design Considerations for SIM Card Holder
