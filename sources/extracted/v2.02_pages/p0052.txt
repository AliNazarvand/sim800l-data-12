4.19. Antenna Interface 
 
There are three antenna ports for SIM800H&SIM800 L, GSM antenna port named GSM_ANT, Bluetooth 
antenna port named BT_ANT and FM antenna port named FM_ANTP/ FM_ANTN, The RF interface of the 
three antenna ports has an impedance of 50Ω. 
z The input impendence of the antenna should be 50Ω, and the VSWR should be less than 2. 
z It is recommended that the GSM antenna and the BT antenna should be placed as far as possible.  
z The isolations of the three antenna should be bigger than 30dB 
NOTE：About the RF trace layout please refer to“AN_SMT Module_RF_Reference Design_Guide”.
