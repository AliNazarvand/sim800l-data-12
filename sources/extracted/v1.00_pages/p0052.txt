4.19. Antenna Interface 
 
There are three antenna ports for SIM800L, GSM antenna port named RF_ANT, antenna port named BT_ANT 
and FM antenna port named FM_ANT_P/ FM_ANT_N, The RF interface of the three antenna ports has an 
impedance of 50Ω. 
z The input impendence of the antenna should be 50Ω, and the VSWR should be less than 2. 
z It is recommended that the GSM antenna and the BT antenna should be placed as far as possible.  
z The isolations of the three antenna should be bigger than 30db
