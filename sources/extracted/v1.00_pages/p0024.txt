When power on procedure is completed, SIM800L will send following URC to indicate that the module is ready

RDY 
This URC does not appear when autobauding function is active. 
 
Note: User can use AT command “AT+IPR=x” to set a fixed baud rate and save  the configuration to

received from the serial port every time when SIM800L is powered on. For details, please refer to the chapter 
“AT+IPR” in document [1]. 
 
4.2.2. Power down SIM800L 
 
SIM800L will be powered down in the following situations： 
z Normal power down procedure: power down SIM800L by the PWRKEY pin.  
z Normal power down procedure: power down SIM800L by AT command “AT+CPOWD=1”.  
z Abnormal power down: over-voltage or under-voltage automatic power down. 
z Abnormal power down: over-temperature or under-temperature automatic power down. 
4.2.2.1. Power down SIM800L by the PWRKEY Pin 
User can power down SIM800L by pulling down the PWRKEY pin for at least 1 second and release. Please refer
